Synthetic Investment Mandate (teaching data; not a real fund mandate)
Effective date: 2026-01-01.
This mandate applies continuously to daily portfolio valuations and simulated post-trade checks. The portfolio is USD-denominated and long-only; cash, equities and ordinary bonds are permitted.
NAV equals cash plus non-cash security market values. All limits include the boundary value.
Cash is not issuer or sector exposure. Issuer and sector classifications come from reference data for the valuation date.
Reference data may be up to 30 calendar days old. Future-dated, unknown or missing information cannot pass automatically.
Ratings use a single AAA-to-D symbol scale; ratings from different agencies are not combined.
ESG flags define a synthetic teaching exclusion list, not an assessment of real companies.
R1: Combined equity and bond market value for any single issuer must not exceed 25% of portfolio NAV; aggregate all securities of the same issuer.
R2: Combined equity and bond market value for any single sector must not exceed 40% of portfolio NAV.
R3: Cash must be at least 5% of portfolio NAV.
R4: Holding the security with identifier TOBACCO is prohibited.
R5: Holding securities of asset type Future, Option or Swap is prohibited.
R6: Ordinary bonds use the single rating field defined by this mandate. The minimum rating is BBB-; ratings below BBB- require remediation.
R7: Securities with esg_excluded=true in the holdings reference table are prohibited; a missing flag must not be treated as a pass.
